Soundling voice lab

A private listening page for choosing a voice. Not part of the app. Nothing is sent anywhere: ratings stay in this browser until you press "Copy my ratings" and paste them back.

Everything here is machine-generated and was built by an AI model that cannot hear audio. Measurements (spectrograms, a speech recogniser) guided the build, but they say nothing reliable about which voice sounds natural: that is the point of this page. Loudness is matched across candidates so a louder clip does not win unfairly. Some clips are experiments and some are known to be wrong; those are marked.

Candidates (untick to hide a candidate; give each an overall 1-5 score at the end)

Rating scale: 1 = robotic / unusable, 3 = acceptable, 5 = I would happily let a 4-year-old hear this all day. Leave a rating blank if you did not listen.

Letter sounds (what a child hears when a letter is tapped)

"c" and "k" are the same sound /k/ (two takes where the voice varies). "direct" clips ask the voice to say the lone sound with no help: they are here to show that this fails.

Words

our phonemes = the app's method (we dictate the sounds). plain text = the voice reads the spelling. in sentence = "I can see the ...".

Flowing speech

"Can you hear the sound sss in sun?" Piper candidates: the phrase is spoken by the voice and that voice's own /s/ clip is spliced in. Azure: one request, with the /s/ given as a lengthened IPA phoneme. Kokoro: not built (a lone /s/ is not available from it).
